The Problem

Many children, elderly people, and individuals with medical conditions may get lost or require urgent assistance. During emergencies, it is often difficult for bystanders or first responders to identify who they are or how to help them quickly.

Our Solution

OmniBand is a smart wearable wristband designed for maximum safety. It stores critical medical information, personal emergency contacts, and live GPS location, offering instant access and alerts when every second counts.

Key Objectives

  • Improve overall personal safety
  • Help emergency responders act faster
  • Enable rapid identification during crises
  • Provide families with continuous peace of mind

Core Features

Emergency SOS Button

Sends immediate location alerts to saved contacts.

GPS Tracking

Provides accurate real-time location sharing.

Medical Data Storage

Holds allergies, conditions, and emergency contact details.

Comfortable Strap

Waterproof, durable teal rubber band.

Long-Lasting Battery

Optimized power consumption for multi-day use.

Concept Evaluation

Idea 1: Safety Necklace

Pros Easy to wear, stores data.
Cons Easily lost, unsafe for sports.

Idea 2: Emergency Badge

Pros Highly visible to others.
Cons Can fall off, limited space for tech.

Idea 3: OmniBand (Chosen)

Pros Comfortable, secure, multi-featured.
Cons Requires charging battery.

Design Challenges & Solutions

Challenges Encountered:
  • Selecting the most critical features without clutter.
  • Keeping operation simple under panic situations.
  • Designing an aesthetically pleasing, modern look.
How We Solved Them:
  • Collaborated to merge the best ideas into one concept.
  • Eliminated non-essential features for clarity.
  • Iterated on visual designs using team feedback.
